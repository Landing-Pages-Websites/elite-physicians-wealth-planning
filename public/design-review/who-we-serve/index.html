# who-we-serve — design decomposition notes

Canonical audience: the professional audience index. These notes assemble the approved final section frames; they do not authorize redesign, invented claims, named-person imagery, or visitor-facing production notes.

## Global assembly rules

- Keep all website copy, route labels, buttons, disclosures, decision tables, icons and connector labels live and semantic.
- Use Cormorant Garamond for editorial display roles and Inter for body, controls and compact labels.
- Use only navy `#0B1F3A`, restrained gold `#C8A65A`, mist `#DCE8F2`, ivory `#F6F2E8`, white and charcoal. Gold lines are one sparse section-local coordination gesture, not a branching network.
- Size every section and media slot before placing the image. `img` intrinsic dimensions may not define grid rows or section height.
- Extracted files contain photography or detailed architectural artwork only. No full section, website heading, button or route table is baked into a raster.
- Desktop proof targets are 1440x900 and 1536x1024; mobile proof target is 390x844. Recompose connectors and media rather than shrinking a desktop screenshot.
